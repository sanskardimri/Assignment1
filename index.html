How NeuroFlow transforms unstructured data into business outcomes in a single, continuous loop.

Step 01 — Ingest

Unified Data Ingestion

Connect any data source — structured databases, event streams, document stores, or third-party APIs — through 200+ native connectors. ALTsrhu normalizes and indexes everything in real time, no ETL pipelines required.

Step 02 — Understand

Neural Context Mapping

Our foundation models build a living semantic graph of your business — relationships, patterns, and causal structures — updating continuously as new data flows in. This is the intelligence layer that makes everything downstream possible.

Step 03 — Decide

Continuous Self-Optimization

Outcomes feed back into the learning loop. ALTsrhu models drift-detect, auto-retrain, and validate before promoting to production — so accuracy compounds over time without human intervention.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How quickly can we go from sign-up to production?
+
Most teams deploy their first production workflow within 48 hours. Our onboarding team provides a dedicated integration engineer for the first 30 days on Growth and Enterprise plans. We also offer pre-built templates for 50+ common enterprise use cases.
Does altsrhu support on-premise or VPC deployments?
+
Yes. Enterprise customers can deploy NeuroFlow in their own VPC on AWS, GCP, or Azure, or on-premise using our Kubernetes-based distribution. Data never leaves your infrastructure boundary. Air-gapped deployments are also supported.
Can we bring our own models (BYOM)?
+
Absolutely.ALTsrhu is model-agnostic. Import any PyTorch, TensorFlow, JAX, or ONNX model and immediately benefit from our inference optimization, versioning, monitoring, and agent orchestration layers — no code changes required.
What compliance certifications do you hold?
+
ALTsrhu holds SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and HIPAA attestations. We're GDPR and CCPA compliant by design. Enterprise customers receive full compliance documentation and can request custom data processing agreements.
How does billing work for variable inference volumes?
+
Plans include a base request allocation. Beyond that, usage is billed at per-request rates that decrease with volume. Enterprise plans can lock in committed-use discounts up to 45% off on-demand pricing. No surprise bills — configurable spend caps are available on all plans.
What support options are available?
+
Starter: community forum and documentation. Growth: email and Slack with 8-hour SLA. Enterprise: 24/7 phone, dedicated Slack channel, named customer success manager, and optional professional services engagements.
